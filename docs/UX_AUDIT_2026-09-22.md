# UX audit and elevation plan — 22 September 2026

First principles for a private money app: the person wants to know **what is left**, **what just happened**, and **what to do next**, in under two seconds, without reading a paragraph. Every screen should lead with one figure or one action, carry status in a glyph plus a short label (never colour alone), and keep explanation one glyph away. Financial precision is never traded for polish: exact amounts, currencies and dates stay in text with monospaced digits.

## Next, in priority order

1. **Today hero.** Lead with one figure: *left this month* across pinned categories, with the day's share underneath, and move the date row's ⓘ onto the header line. The pinned board becomes the detail beneath the figure.
2. **History rows.** A small category tint dot beside the kind glyph and a right-aligned amount with the currency code muted, so scanning a day is a single vertical line of numbers.
3. **Plan → Budget.** Replace the *Spending · month marker* legend row with an inline caption under the bar and make the pace segment control icon-plus-label only when selected (same rule as the scope chips).
4. **Assets.** Group accounts by currency with a per-currency subtotal row; move *Save today's snapshot* to the toolbar as a camera glyph with a label on tap confirmation.
5. **Success feedback.** After Save in Log, animate the saved amount into the *Saved* banner (spring, Reduce Motion aware) so the eye follows the money; today the banner appears without motion.
6. **Onboarding.** Cut each step's card copy to one line per benefit; the illustrations already carry the mood.
7. **Motion.** Adopt the shared `MoneyUpMotion.disclosure` spring on all expandable cards; today a few use the default easing.
8. **Large text.** Verify the new History scope row and Log currency menu at accessibility sizes on device (the render tests cover XL text for Insights and Log).

## Engineering items found on the way

- `DeveloperSupportTests` (StoreKit purchase) is timing-dependent on the simulator's StoreKit daemon and fails intermittently in CI; it should poll the transaction stream with a bounded deadline instead of fixed sleeps.
- `appstore_release.py prepare` cannot replace the build of a version in `WAITING_FOR_REVIEW`; add an explicit, confirmed *withdraw and resubmit* operation so the owner can do this from the workflow.
- Apple needs about eight minutes after upload before a build is API-visible; the tester workflow could poll `inspect` before `inherit-compliance` instead of failing.
- The Paid Apps Agreement is recorded as not accepted; live tips cannot complete until the Account Holder accepts it in App Store Connect.
